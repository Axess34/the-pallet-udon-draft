About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

A music space in the middle of Udon.

THE PALLET has been a music space & art exhibition since 2013 — a place for bands, friends and long nights in Udon Thani. Glass walls, warm wood, string lights and a stage at the heart of the room.

Sit inside close to the band, or out on the terrace under the lights. Come early to chill, stay late for the music.

“บรรยากาศดี เหมาะสำหรับนั่งชิวไป ฟังเพลงสบายๆ”
  • Live bands in the evening
  • Indoor bar & outdoor terrace seating
  • Football on the big screens

Live music · ดนตรีสด

Turn it up. Tonight, live.

Local bands take the stage in the evening — from easy acoustic sets to full-band rock. Between sets, the playlist keeps going.

Weekends get busy, especially after 9 PM. Planning a night out with a group? Call ahead and we’ll save you a table.
